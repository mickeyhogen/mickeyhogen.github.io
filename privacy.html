Privacy Policy — Mickey Mail Guard

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What the app accesses

Only the Gmail account of its single consenting user, through the Google OAuth scopes gmail.readonly (read messages) and gmail.send (send messages). No other Google data is requested.

How the data is used

To show the user summaries of correspondence the user has selected, and to send emails the user has approved. Nothing else.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed on the user's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally with owner-only file permissions. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties, except that outgoing emails are delivered through Gmail.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; changing the Google account password also revokes it.
